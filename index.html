Research

Property refinement and specification debt

Formal specifications grow with every design iteration, and many temporal properties end up implied by others. I develop property refinement for Linear Temporal Logic (LTL) and Computation Tree Logic (CTL): a semantic relation that identifies redundant or subsumed properties and computes a minimal sufficient set to model-check, reducing verification effort and exposing dependencies between requirements.

Timed refinement for evolving real-time systems

Post-deployment updates to real-time and safety-critical systems must preserve what was verified before. With Relaxed Weak Timed Bisimulation (RWTBS), updates are checked compositionally: output deadlines are preserved strictly while input reception may be relaxed in a controlled way. Building on this, I study correct-by-construction evolution of digital twins.

Self-adaptive and socially compliant autonomous systems

Autonomous systems must adapt at runtime and behave acceptably around people. I work on runtime self-optimization that keeps exact optimality through knowledge-driven design space reduction, and on verifying social norms alongside safety and liveness with reusable (T)CTL observer patterns.
